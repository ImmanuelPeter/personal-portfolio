A. Personal introduction

Computer Science Student

I'm Immanuel Gondwe, a third-year Bachelor of Science in Computer Science student at Dmi-st.john the baptist university mangochi campus malawi.

My interest in technology started with with how computer systems works. That curiosity took me from editing my first HTML page, to writing object-oriented c++, python and Java programs, designing relational databases and building web applications. Today I am most motivated by work that connects software engineering fundamentals with real people and real constraints.

I enjoy the full shape of a project: understanding the problem, sketching a data model, building the interface and then testing it with someone who will actually use it. Some of my academic interests include software development, artificial intelligence, networking and cybersecurity, Web development, UI and UX Design — and I am always looking for the overlap between them.

Outside the lecture theatre, I know Graphic Designing, Videography, Communication, Teaching digital skills and my aim is to contribute meaningfully to the field of technology. Each rebuild teaches me a pattern I didn't fully understand the first time.

E. Career aspirations

What I aspire to become

A practical plan for growing into an engineer who contributes.

My goal is to become a Computer Scientist and IT Professional I want my work to be measured by who it helps, not only by how computer systems operates.

  1. Complete my degree with a strong foundation Finish my BSc in Computer Science with solid grades in algorithms, databases, software engineering, networks and computer science general principles.
  2. Start my career as a Computer Scientist Join a team where I can ship real features, learn from code review and grow into a dependable full-stack developer.
  3. Specialise in artificial intelligence and secure computer systems Build expertise in applied machine learning while keeping security, privacy and responsible use at the centre of every design.
  4. Use technology to serve my community Contribute to tools for education, agriculture and financial inclusion — and mentor the next group of students following the same path.
